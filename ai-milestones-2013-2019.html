Overview

I contributed content covering AI developments from 2013 through 2019 to a group timeline presentation. My section covers changes in language processing, generative models, reinforcement learning, and deep neural networks.

The project

The contribution appears on two slides: “AI Development: 2013–2015” and “AI Development: 2017–2019.” Each pairs dated milestones with short explanations of the development and its significance.

My contribution

I contributed the content for the presentation’s 2013–2019 section, shown on slides 8 and 9. The complete timeline is a group project.

The goal

Explain a period of AI development in a clear chronological format, connecting important models and tools with the capabilities they introduced. This section contributes to the group’s broader historical timeline.

My approach

  1. Work within my section. I contributed the content for the 2013–2019 section on slides 8 and 9. This kept my work focused on one part of the group’s wider AI timeline.
  2. Use the group’s research process. The original presentation records the group’s use of generative AI to gather background information, identify milestones, and organize the content. Its disclosure states that information was reviewed and cross-referenced with additional sources.
  3. Organize and explain the milestones. My section pairs dates with short explanations of what changed and why it mattered, moving from word representations and generative networks to Transformers and language models. The chronological structure helps introductory readers follow the developments.
  4. Adapt the section for the portfolio. With AI assistance, I turned the slide fragments into complete sentences, checked the selected dates and explanations against original papers and announcements, and added a source link for each milestone. I kept the original group work separate and made my contribution explicit.

Tools & technologies

  • Microsoft PowerPoint: the final group timeline is a .pptx presentation; my content appears on slides 8 and 9.
  • Generative AI: the group’s original disclosure records assistance with background research, milestone discovery, and organization. AI also assisted with source checks and wording revisions for this portfolio version.
  • Original research papers and announcements: supporting sources for the selected dates and explanations, linked beside each milestone.
  • HTML and CSS: the web page structure and presentation used to make my section readable outside the slide deck.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting for the portfolio entry and its complete content excerpt.

Why it matters

What makes it useful

The section turns a sequence of technical milestones into short explanations that readers can scan alongside dates. It provides context for how language processing and other AI capabilities developed during this period.

Who it helps

Students and readers new to AI can use the dated summaries and source links as a starting point for understanding developments behind current AI tools. The artifact demonstrates my ability to make technical material easier to work with through clear explanations, supporting my broader focus on useful software and applied AI.
