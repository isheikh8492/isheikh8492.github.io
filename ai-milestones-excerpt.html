AI Milestones: 2013–2019

Adapted from my content contribution to slides 8–9 of the AIML-500 group timeline.

This web version improves sentence clarity while retaining the selected milestones and dates. The full presentation is a group project. Dates identify the linked research submissions or announcements; this selection does not cover every development in the period.

January 16, 2013

word2vec research

Introduced efficient ways to represent words as numerical vectors, helping models capture relationships between words.

Source: word2vec — Efficient Estimation of Word Representations in Vector Space

June 10, 2014

Generative adversarial networks (GANs)

Introduced a training approach with two competing networks: one generates examples, while the other learns to distinguish generated examples from real data.

Source: Generative Adversarial Nets

September 1, 2014

Attention in machine translation

Allowed a translation model to focus on relevant parts of the source sentence when predicting each target word, addressing a limitation of encoding an entire sentence in one fixed-length vector.

Source: Neural Machine Translation by Jointly Learning to Align and Translate

February 25, 2015

DeepMind’s Atari breakthrough

Showed how a deep Q-network could learn to play Atari games from screen pixels and rewards, using the same network architecture across different games.

Source: From Pixels to Actions — Deep Reinforcement Learning

November 9, 2015

TensorFlow becomes open source

Made Google’s machine learning system publicly available, allowing researchers and developers to share working code and build on common tools.

Source: TensorFlow becomes open source

December 10, 2015

ResNet research

Introduced residual learning to make much deeper neural networks easier to train, with strong results in image recognition.

Source: Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition

December 11, 2015

OpenAI announced

Introduced an organization focused on advancing AI research and its benefits for humanity.

Source: Introducing OpenAI

June 12, 2017

Transformers

Introduced a model architecture based on attention, enabling more parallel processing and faster training than the recurrent approaches compared in the paper.

Source: Attention Is All You Need

December 5, 2017

AlphaZero research

Showed that a reinforcement learning system could reach strong performance in chess, shogi, and Go through self-play, starting with the rules of each game.

Source: AlphaZero research

June 11, 2018

Original GPT announced

Combined unsupervised pretraining on text with supervised fine-tuning for specific language tasks. This helped demonstrate how a broadly pretrained model could be adapted to different tasks.

Source: Improving Language Understanding with Unsupervised Learning

October 11, 2018

BERT research

Introduced pretraining that uses context from both sides of a word, producing representations that can be fine-tuned for language tasks.

Source: BERT research

February 14, 2019

GPT-2 announced

Demonstrated coherent text generation and raised concerns about potential misuse of generated text, influencing the model’s staged release.

Source: Better Language Models and Their Implications
