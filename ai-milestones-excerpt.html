AI Milestones: Slide Content

Submitted text from my 2013–2019 contribution, presented here in a readable web format. Original slide design and group credits remain in the full presentation.

Slide 8 · AI DEVELOPMENT: 2013–2015

January 16, 2013

Google’s word2vec model

Helped computers find relationships between

words improving the way AI processed language.

June 10, 2014

Generative adversarial networks (GANs)

Introduced a way for AI to create new images and

other data. Two networks improve together: one

creates examples, and the other checks them.

September 1, 2014

Attention in machine translation

Helped AI focus on the most useful words in a

sentence. Easier for working with longer

sentences

February 25, 2015

DeepMind’s Atari breakthrough

Showed that AI could learn to play using a

practice and rewards system, helping AI self-learn

November 9, 2015

TensorFlow becomes open source

More access to tools that help build with AI

December 10, 2015

ResNet research

Made it easier for neural networks for many layers

to be trained increasing Image recognition

December 11, 2015

OpenAI announced

Org focused on AI research

Slide 9 · AI DEVELOPMENT: 2017–2019

June 12, 2017

Transformers

Efficient way to process language during training

December 5, 2017

AlphaZero research

AI could learn by playing games with itself

June 11, 2018

Original GPT announced

AI could learn patterns from text and then be

trained on that text for tasks.

October 11, 2018

BERT research

Helped AI use the words before and after a word

to work out its meaning

February 14, 2019

GPT-2 announced

Showed that AI could generate longer, more

connected passages of text.

It also increased discussion about how

AI-generated text could be misused.
